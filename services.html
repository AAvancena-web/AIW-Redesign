Roofs and Decks

Flat Roof Membranes

Flat roofs and decks take the worst of the weather. We specify and install a system that suits your design, your falls and your budget, and we detail the junctions that actually cause the leaks.

  • Torch on, TPO single ply or liquid applied, matched to the build
  • Upstands, penetrations and gutter junctions detailed properly
  • Flood testing where the design calls for it
  • Manufacturer backed warranty registered in your name
Enquire About Flat Roof Membranes
Basements and Foundations

Below Ground Tanking

Tanking has to be right the first time, because there is no second chance once the backfill goes in. It is an integral part of the foundation, so we treat it that way.

  • Sheet and liquid systems for block and poured concrete
  • Water stop installed to every construction joint
  • Drainage and protection board where required
  • Staged around your pours so the programme keeps moving
Enquire About Below Ground Tanking
Construction Joints

Water Stop Systems

A water stop is the seal cast inside the concrete itself. It is what stops water tracking through a joint once the building is finished, and it is invisible the moment the pour goes in.

  • PVC water stop to construction and expansion joints
  • Hydrophilic strip to slab to wall junctions and pour breaks
  • Detailed together with the tanking as one system
  • Checked and signed off before every pour
Enquire About Water Stop Systems
Modified Bitumen

Torch On Membranes

Modified bitumen sheets with generous overlaps. A durable, proven answer for roofs, decks, basements, roof gardens and planter boxes where the system has to last.

  • Two layer systems for the longest design life
  • Generous laps that hold up under ponding
  • Suits roofs, decks, basements and planters
  • Applied by crews who have done it for years
Enquire About Torch On Membranes
Thermoplastic Polyolefin

TPO Single Ply

Thermoplastic polyolefin is heat welded at the seams, free of toxic plasticisers and highly reflective. A clean, light and sustainable roofing answer for larger areas.

  • Hot air welded seams, no solvents or adhesives at the laps
  • Reflective surface that keeps the space below cooler
  • Light enough for roofs with limited load capacity
  • Recyclable and free of toxic plasticisers
Enquire About TPO Single Ply
Remedial and Detailing

Liquid Membranes

Seamless coatings for remedial and maintenance work, applied over timber, concrete and a range of existing substrates. Where the detailing is awkward, liquid usually wins.

  • Fully bonded and seamless, no laps to fail
  • Goes over timber, concrete and many existing surfaces
  • Ideal for plant rooms, small decks and tricky geometry
  • Good answer for remedial work without a full strip out
Enquire About Liquid Membranes
Roof Gardens and Planters

Green Roof Systems

Roof gardens carry deep soil, mature planting and irrigation. We protect the structure and the living spaces underneath with a root resistant build up designed for the load.

  • Root resistant membranes that will not be breached by planting
  • Drainage and filter layers specified for the build up
  • Planter box and raised bed waterproofing
  • Coordinated with the landscaper before anything goes down

How We Work

Four Steps From First Call to Signed Off Warranty

A clear process means no surprises for your programme and no gaps in the detailing.

Site Assessment
