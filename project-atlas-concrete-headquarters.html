Project Overview

Atlas Concrete Headquarters

Around 1000 square metres of Nuraply 3PM double layer torch on membrane, on a building where the membrane is the finished surface and is meant to be looked at.

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing completed approximately 1000 square metres of Nuraply 3PM double layer torch on membrane system.

Several challenges on this project involved design compliance, buildability and maintaining a high standard of appearance. The flat roof areas needed particular attention, because there the membrane forms the final visible finish and so had to achieve both the performance and the look.

On This Job

What Had to Be Solved

Every roof has something. These are the ones that shaped how this job was run.

The Challenge

The Membrane Is the Finish

On the flat roof areas the membrane is what you see, so a sound roof was only half the job.

Additional care went into the installation to keep the membrane neatly detailed, consistently finished and aligned with the architectural intent. With the project recognised as an award winning development, the appearance of the roofing mattered as much as its performance.

The Challenge

Compliance and Buildability Together

Design compliance and buildability had to be solved at the same time, not traded off against each other.

We liaised closely with the site team through construction to identify potential buildability issues early and make sure the flat roof areas could be built in accordance with the design requirements.

The Challenge

Advice as the Build Progressed

Rather than wait for problems to appear at the end, we stayed in the conversation throughout.

That ongoing coordination let us work through design and installation challenges as they came up, while holding compliance, buildability, quality and the overall architectural outcome.

What Went Onto This Roof

1000 m2 of Nuraply 3PM and 3PG ballast roof.

Flat roof

Nuraply 3PM, Double Layer Torch On

Two layers of torch on modified bitumen over plywood and concrete, detailed as a finished surface.

  • Approximately 1000 square metres
  • Double layer for design life and redundancy
  • Laid over plywood and concrete substrates
  • Detailed to suit a visible finished surface
Ballast areas

Nuraply 3PG

The 3PG sheet where the roof build up calls for a ballasted finish over the membrane.

  • Ballast roof system where specified
  • Membrane protected by the build up above
  • Junctions between systems detailed by hand
  • Finish aligned with the architectural intent
