Flat Roof Waterproofing in Auckland

A flat roof is the hardest surface on a building to keep watertight, because water sits on it rather than running off. We specify and install the system that suits your falls, your substrate and your design life, then detail the junctions that actually cause the leaks.

Most flat roof failures are not failures of the sheet in the middle of the roof. They happen at the upstands, the penetrations, the gutter junctions and the thresholds, where the membrane has to change direction and meet something else. That is where we spend the time.

We work in torch on modified bitumen, TPO single ply and liquid applied systems, and we will tell you which one we would put on your roof and why. The answer depends on the falls, the traffic the surface has to take, the weight the structure can carry and what the finished surface has to look like.

How We Do It

What Goes Into a Flat Roof That Lasts

The parts of the job nobody sees, which are the parts that decide how long it lasts.

The detailing

Junctions, Not Fields

Every change of direction is a potential entry point, so each one is set out and reinforced by hand.

  • Upstands taken to height, not just turned up
  • Penetrations collared and reinforced
  • Internal gutters lapped clear of the water line
  • Thresholds coordinated with the joinery
The system

Chosen for the Roof, Not the Price List

We match the membrane to the falls, the traffic and the load the structure can take.

  • Torch on where durability and lap width matter
  • TPO where weight and heat gain matter
  • Liquid where the geometry is awkward
  • Flood testing where the design calls for it

Commonly Asked

Flat Roof Membranes, Answered Straight

The questions we get asked most about this system. There are more on the FAQ page.

Which membrane is best for a flat roof?

There is no single answer. Torch on suits roofs and decks that need durability and generous laps. TPO single ply is light, reflective and heat welded. Liquid membranes handle tricky detailing and remedial work. We match the system to your falls, your penetrations and your finish.

How long does a flat roof membrane last?

A correctly specified and correctly installed system will generally give you fifteen to twenty five years, and longer where it is protected by paving, ballast or a green roof build up. We will tell you the expected life of whatever we quote.

Can you waterproof a deck that will be tiled or paved over?

Yes, and it is common. The membrane goes down first and the finish is built over it, which protects the membrane and usually extends its life. The detail that matters most is the threshold where the deck meets the building.

My flat roof is leaking. Do you do repairs?

Yes. We find where the water is actually getting in, which is often not where it shows up inside, then repair or overlay with a system suited to the existing substrate.
