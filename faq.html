Before You Start

Quoting and Getting Started

What a price from us covers, what we need from you, and how the first conversation usually goes.

How much does waterproofing cost in Auckland?

It depends on the area, the substrate, access to the site and the system the detail calls for, so we do not quote off a square metre rate sight unseen. We walk the site or read the plans, then give you a scoped, itemised price with the membrane system named, so you can compare it fairly against anyone else.

How do I get a quote from you?

Call the office or send us the plans and we will come back to you. For anything with tricky detailing we would rather see the site than guess from a drawing, and a site visit costs you nothing. You get the price in writing with the system, the areas and the inclusions set out.

Is the site assessment really free?

Yes. We will come out, look at what you have, and tell you what we think it needs. If the honest answer is that you do not need us yet, or that another trade should go first, we will say so.

What do you need from me before you can price it?

Plans and specifications if you have them, the programme dates you are working to, and access details for the site. If it is remedial work, anything you can tell us about when and where the water shows up is genuinely useful.

Can you price a job from plans alone?

Often yes, on new builds where the detailing is drawn and the areas are clear. Where a plan leaves the junctions open to interpretation, we will flag the assumptions we have priced on rather than bury them.

When should I get you involved on a new build?

Earlier than most people do. Waterproofing decisions get made by the falls, the upstand heights and the penetration layout, and those are cheap to change on paper and expensive to change on site.

Systems and Materials

Membranes, and Which One You Need

Torch on, TPO, liquid and green roof build ups all do different jobs. Here is how we choose between them.

Which system is best for a flat roof or deck?

There is no single answer. Torch on suits roofs and decks that need durability and generous laps. TPO single ply is light, reflective and heat welded. Liquid membranes handle tricky detailing and remedial work. We match the system to your falls, your penetrations and your finish.

How long does a waterproofing membrane last?

A correctly specified and correctly installed system will generally give you fifteen to twenty five years, and longer where it is protected by paving, ballast or a green roof build up. Design life depends on the product, the exposure and the detailing, and we will tell you the expected life of whatever we quote.

What is a torch on membrane?

Modified bitumen sheet, bonded down with a gas torch as it is rolled out. It is laid in one or two layers depending on the detail, and it is the workhorse of flat roof and deck waterproofing here because the laps are generous and the material is tough enough to take traffic during the build.

What is TPO single ply, and when would you use it?

A thin thermoplastic sheet that is hot air welded at the seams rather than torched. It is light, it reflects heat rather than absorbing it, and it suits large simple roof areas where the weight of a bitumen system is a problem.

What are liquid membranes for?

Anywhere sheet material is awkward. Complicated penetrations, plant bases, small balconies, and remedial work over a substrate that will not take a torch. A liquid system goes on seamless and takes the shape of whatever it is covering.

Can you install a green roof?

Yes. The waterproofing under a green roof has to be root resistant and it has to be right the first time, because everything above it has to come off to reach it. We install the membrane and the root barrier and coordinate with whoever is doing the planting above.

Below Ground

Tanking and Water Stop

Basements, garages and anything sitting in wet ground. This is the work that is cheapest to do well and dearest to do twice.

What is the difference between a membrane and a water stop?

A membrane sits on the surface and keeps water off the structure. A water stop is cast inside the concrete at a joint, so water cannot track through the joint itself. Below ground you usually need both, and they have to be detailed together to work as one system.

When does tanking need to go in?

Before the backfill and before the slab pour, while every face is still open. Once the ground goes back and the concrete is down, the membrane is buried, and the sequence is the whole point of the job.

My garage is below ground level. Do I need tanking?

Almost certainly. Ground conditions vary enormously from one site to the next, and a below grade garage spends its life in contact with whatever the ground is holding. Tanking it turns the water away from the concrete rather than asking the finished floor to stop it.

What happens if a below ground leak is found later?

It becomes an expensive problem. Reaching a buried membrane means excavating back to it, and the cost of that has nothing to do with the cost of doing the waterproofing properly in the first place. That is why we push so hard on getting the sequence right.

Can you work around a staged pour?

Yes, and most tanking and water stop jobs are staged. We put the water stop in at each construction joint, sign that stage off, and come back for the next one so your pours are not waiting on us.

Leaks and Remedial Work

When Something Is Already Failing

A good share of what we do is fixing waterproofing that somebody else put down. Here is how that usually goes.

Do you fix existing leaks, or only new builds?

Both. A good share of our work is remedial. We find where the water is actually getting in, which is often not where it shows up inside, then repair or overlay with a liquid or sheet system suited to the existing substrate.

My flat roof membrane is bubbling and lifting. What does that mean?

Usually that it was not applied correctly, or that moisture was trapped underneath it when it went down. Bubbling and lifting are symptoms rather than the problem, and they tell us where to look. It is worth dealing with before the water finds its way through.

Can you lay a new membrane straight over an old one?

Only once the old one is sound. We assess what is there first and make good the failures, because a new system laid over a failing one will fail in the same places. Where the substrate is right, overlaying is often the sensible option.

The leak shows up in one place. Is that where the problem is?

Often not. Water travels along falls, laps and framing before it appears inside, so the wet patch on the ceiling and the hole in the membrane can be metres apart. Finding the actual entry point is most of the work on a remedial job.

Is it worth repairing, or should the whole area be redone?

It depends on how much of the system has gone and how old it is. We will tell you honestly which one we would do if it were ours, and price both where both are genuinely on the table.

Compliance and Working With Us

Paperwork, Programmes and Coverage

Producer statements, warranties, how we fit around your build, and where in Auckland we work.

Do you supply producer statements for council?

Yes. We issue a producer statement for the waterproofing on completion and register the manufacturer warranty for the system we installed, so your council paperwork and your warranty both line up.

Are you Licensed Building Practitioners?

Yes. We are Licensed Building Practitioners and our crews are Site Safe accredited, and we hold approved applicator status with the major New Zealand membrane suppliers, which is what makes the manufacturer warranty on your system stand up.

Do you belong to an industry body?

We are members of Membrane Group New Zealand, the industry association for membrane roofing and waterproofing applicators here.

Can you work to a staged build programme?

Yes. Tanking and water stop usually go in across several pours, so we stage our crews around your programme and sign off each stage before the next trade starts. Keeping the next trade off our back is part of the job.

Do you work outside central Auckland?

Yes. We cover the wider Auckland region, from the North Shore through the central suburbs and eastern bays, out west and south to Manukau and Franklin. Tell us where the site is and we will confirm before we quote.

How long have you been doing this?

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing has been going since 2012, and the trade experience behind it runs to more than twenty years of construction waterproofing.
