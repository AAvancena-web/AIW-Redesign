A refurbished warehouse and a new build under one roof package, with a full insulated warm roof over the existing structure and a double layer torch on system over the new.

Project Overview

Life Church, NZ

Life NZ was moving venues and had the opportunity to build on a site in Mt Eden, Auckland. Part of the build was a refurbishment of an existing warehouse as well as a new build. A careful thought process had to be considered when working with the many service penetrations through the existing and the new roof.

In order to reduce heat loss, Nuralite Nuratherm was used to provide a full layer of insulation on top of the existing roof. Nuratherm is made up of two layers of waterproofing, PIR insulation and a vapour barrier. The new roof received a double layer torch on Nuralite 3PM.

The Challenge

Two Roofs, One System

An existing warehouse roof and a new build had to end up as a single watertight envelope, and the existing roof carried a large number of service penetrations that all had to be worked around rather than removed.

The Solution

A Warm Roof Over the Old

A Nuratherm warm roof build up went over the existing structure to cut heat loss, with every penetration detailed by hand. The new roof took a double layer torch on membrane, so both halves finished to the same standard.

What Went On the Roof

Two different build ups, chosen for what each half of the building needed.

Existing warehouse

Nuralite Nuratherm Warm Roof

A full layer of insulation laid over the existing roof to cut heat loss from the building below.

  • Two layers of waterproofing
  • PIR insulation board
  • Vapour barrier beneath the build up
  • Every service penetration detailed by hand
New build

Nuralite 3PM Torch On

A double layer torch on membrane over the new roof, matched to the standard of the refurbished side.

  • Double layer modified bitumen
  • Generous laps, fully bonded
  • Upstands and penetrations detailed to match
  • Manufacturer backed warranty registered
