Find Out About Auckland Integrity Waterproofing

One of Auckland's Largest Waterproofing Application Companies

We are Licensed Building Practitioners with over twenty years of construction waterproofing experience within the industry. Whether it is a large commercial project or a small residential job, every project is different, and we use that knowledge to deliver the highest standard and quality so our clients end up with the right application solution.

New buildings and homes can be exciting and a bit daunting. The more difficult the design, the more important it is to make sure the water egress design actually works. When it comes to applying that design and laying the materials, we are with you step by step, with the help and support you will need.

We are an approved applicator for the major waterproofing system suppliers and we deal with the major building material suppliers. We are also an associate member of the Membrane Group (MGNZ).

How We Got Here

A Business Built on Getting the Detail Right

We started small and grew on repeat work from builders who kept calling us back. That is still how the business runs.

2012

The Doors Open

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing starts out on residential decks and roofs, with one principle: the detail is the job, and integrity is in the name for a reason.

2015

Into Commercial Work

Builders and project managers start bringing us their commercial tanking packages. Below ground work becomes a core part of what we do, water stop detailing included.

2018

Approved Applicator Status

We take on approved applicator status with the major New Zealand membrane suppliers, so the manufacturer warranty behind every system we install stands up.

Now

One of the Largest in Auckland

More than 855 completed projects, a crew averaging fifteen years in waterproofing, and associate membership of the Membrane Group. Same standard on a small deck as on a large commercial job.
