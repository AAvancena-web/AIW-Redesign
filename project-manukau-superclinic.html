Project Overview

Manukau Superclinic

Derbigum to the roof plant decks and Fibertite to the internal gutters, with custom tapered insulation made to fix falls that had been ponding since the original design.

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing completed approximately 370 square metres of Rooflogic Derbigum Xtreme membrane system to the roof plant deck areas, and 150 square metres of Rooflogic Fibertite Xtreme membrane system to the internal gutter areas.

On This Job

What Had to Be Solved

Every roof has something. These are the ones that shaped how this job was run.

The Challenge

Keeping Going Through the Weather

Adverse weather made the planned installation programme difficult to hold.

Temporary weather protection was installed so the team could keep laying the PIR insulation boards and cover boards without dropping the quality or safety standards, which kept the job on schedule despite the conditions and a tight programme.

The Challenge

Falls That Had Always Ponded

The existing roof falls left areas prone to ponding, a consequence of the original design rather than anything on site.

We worked with the architect and the Rooflogic team to develop and manufacture customised tapered PIR insulation boards, designed to improve the falls and create a drainage path that carries water to the outlets rather than leaving it standing.

The Challenge

Diverters That Were Not on the Drawings

Reviewing the roof drainage and penetration details, we found places where additional water management would give the building more protection.

We proposed additional diverters, not included in the original drawings, positioned to push water away from roof penetrations and towards the designated outlets.

System Installed

What Went Onto This Roof

520 m2 of Rooflogic Derbigum and Fibertite Xtreme.

Plant decks

Derbigum Xtreme, Double Layer

Around 370 square metres of double layer Derbigum over the roof plant deck areas, on acoustic board.

  • Double layer for the redundancy plant decks need
  • Laid over tapered and flat PIR insulation
  • Acoustic board as the substrate
  • Falls corrected in the insulation rather than the structure
Internal gutters

Fibertite Xtreme, Single Layer

Around 150 square metres of Fibertite to the internal gutters, where the detailing matters most.

  • Single layer Fibertite to the gutter runs
  • Outlets and penetrations detailed individually
  • Additional diverters to steer water to the outlets
  • Laps kept clear of the water line
