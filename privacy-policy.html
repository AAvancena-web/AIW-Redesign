Who We Are

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing is a waterproofing contractor based in Remuera, Auckland, working across the wider Auckland region. This policy explains what we do with personal information collected through this website, and applies to aiw.co.nz and every page on it.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 and the information privacy principles it sets out.

To confirm: the registered legal entity name and company number to name here, and whether one person should be listed as the contact for privacy requests.

What We Collect

The only personal information this website asks you for is what you type into an enquiry form. Those forms collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your suburb, which is optional
  • The service you are asking about and the type of project
  • Whatever you write in the message box

Please only put in the message box what you are comfortable sending us by email. There is no need to include anything sensitive in order to get a quote.

Like almost every website, our web server also records ordinary technical information when a page is requested, such as the IP address it was requested from, the browser used and the time of the request. We do not use this to identify individual visitors.

This site does not run analytics, advertising or visitor tracking of any kind.

How We Use It

We use what you send us to do the thing you asked for, which is normally one of:

  • Replying to your enquiry
  • Arranging and carrying out a site assessment
  • Preparing and sending you a quote
  • Carrying out work you engage us for, and the paperwork that goes with it

We do not sell personal information, and we do not add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website does not set cookies of its own, and it does not use cookies to track you between visits or across other sites.

Some of the third party services embedded in these pages may set their own cookies in your browser, which is outside our control. Those services are listed in the next section.

Third Party Services on This Site

A few parts of these pages are loaded from other companies. When your browser fetches them, it makes a request to that company, which means your IP address and basic browser information reach them. We do not receive anything back from them about you.

Google Maps

The map showing our location is embedded from Google Maps. Loading it involves a request to Google, and Google may set cookies. Their handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google reCAPTCHA

Our enquiry forms are protected by reCAPTCHA, which is what stops the forms being flooded with automated spam. Google collects information about your interaction with the page in order to tell a person from a bot, under the same Google Privacy Policy and the Google Terms of Service.

Google Fonts and Cloudflare

The typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, and the icons by Cloudflare. Both receive a request from your browser when a page loads. Neither is used by us to identify or track you.

Who We Share It With

We share your information only where we need to in order to do the work, or where the law requires it. In practice that means:

  • Our own staff and crews working on your job
  • Membrane manufacturers, where a warranty has to be registered in your name
  • The main contractor, builder or project manager where they engaged us, or you asked us to deal with them
  • Our accountant and professional advisers, where they need it
  • Anyone we are legally required to disclose to

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to answer the enquiry and to keep a record of quotes given. Records relating to work we carried out are kept longer, because warranties, producer statements and tax obligations all require it.

To confirm: how long unsuccessful enquiries should be kept before they are deleted, and the retention period for completed job records. Seven years is the usual default for anything with a tax or warranty aspect, but this should be your decision rather than an assumption on the page.

Storage and Security

Enquiries reach us by email and are held in our business email and our job records. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, but no method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information in transit.

Some of the services we use to run the business store data on servers outside New Zealand. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the information has comparable protection to that required by the Privacy Act 2020.

To confirm: which email and job management systems are in use, and whether any of them store data offshore, so this section can name them.

Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to ask us for the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Just email admin@aiw.co.nz or call the office and tell us what you need. We may need to check who you are before we release anything.

There is normally no charge for this, and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can and within the timeframe the Act requires.

If You Are Not Happy

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, please tell us first so we have a chance to put it right. If you are not satisfied with how we respond, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top of it. The version on this page is the one that applies.
