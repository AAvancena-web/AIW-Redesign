Water Stop Systems in Auckland

A water stop is the seal cast inside the concrete itself. It is what stops water tracking through a joint once the building is finished, and it becomes invisible the moment the pour goes in.

Concrete is poured in stages, and every place two pours meet is a construction joint. A joint is a path, and water will find it. A membrane on the outside face cannot help you there, because the water is already inside the structure travelling along the joint.

We install PVC water stop to construction and expansion joints and hydrophilic strip to slab to wall junctions and pour breaks, and we detail it together with the tanking so the two work as one system rather than two trades meeting at a line.

How We Do It

One Chance, Before the Concrete Goes In

A water stop is only as good as its position and its continuity.

Installation

Fixed, Not Just Placed

A profile that moves during the pour is a profile that does not work, so it is fixed and checked in position.

  • Held to the centre line of the joint
  • Continuous around corners and at changes of direction
  • Joins welded rather than overlapped
  • Checked and signed off before every pour
Coordination

Detailed With the Tanking

Below ground the water stop and the membrane are one system, and the place they meet is the place that leaks.

  • Set out alongside the tanking, not after it
  • Transitions between the two detailed by hand
  • Staged around your pour programme
  • Photographed before the concrete covers it

Commonly Asked

Water Stop Systems, Answered Straight

The questions we get asked most about this system. There are more on the FAQ page.

What is the difference between a membrane and a water stop?

A membrane sits on the surface and keeps water off the structure. A water stop is cast inside the concrete at a joint, so water cannot track through the joint itself. Below ground you usually need both, and they have to be detailed together to work as one system.

When does the water stop have to be installed?

Before the pour it sits in. It is cast into the concrete, so once that pour has gone in, the opportunity has passed for that joint.

What is the difference between PVC and hydrophilic water stop?

PVC profiles are cast into the joint and physically block the path. Hydrophilic strip swells on contact with water to seal the gap, and suits slab to wall junctions and pour breaks where a full profile is impractical.

Do you install water stop without doing the tanking?

We can, but we would rather quote both. The junction between the two is the part that decides whether the below ground envelope actually works.
