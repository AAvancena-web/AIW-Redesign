Before You Call

What to Have Ready

None of it is essential, but it gets you a tighter price faster.

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within one working day. If it is urgent, call the office rather than emailing, since the phone gets answered during office hours.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Plans or elevations if you have them, the site address, the approximate area in square metres and your timeframe. Photos help a lot on remedial work.

Do you charge for a site visit?

No. The site assessment and the quote that follows it are free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A small deck gets the same standard of detailing as a large commercial tanking package. Tell us what you have and we will tell you honestly if we are the right fit.
