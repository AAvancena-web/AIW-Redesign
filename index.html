Auckland Waterproofing and Water Stop

Two Systems, One Watertight Result

The membrane keeps water off the structure. The water stop keeps it out of the joints. We install both, and we detail them together.

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing has been doing waterproofing in Auckland since 2012, on new builds, renovations and remedial work.

We install certified membrane systems to flat roofs, decks, balconies, planter boxes and basements, and we carry approved applicator status with the major New Zealand suppliers, so the manufacturer warranty behind your Auckland waterproofing stands up.

We install both PVC and hydrophilic water stop alongside our tanking packages, and we detail the two together so the membrane and the joint seal work as one system rather than two separate trades handing off to each other.

Commonly Asked

Waterproofing Questions, Answered Straight

The things builders and home owners ask us most. If your question is not here, call the office and we will talk it through.

How much does waterproofing cost in Auckland?

It depends on the area, the substrate, access to the site and the system the detail calls for, so we do not quote off a square metre rate sight unseen. We walk the site or read the plans, then give you a scoped, itemised price with the membrane system named, so you can compare it fairly against anyone else.

How long does a waterproofing membrane last?

A correctly specified and correctly installed system will generally give you fifteen to twenty five years, and longer where it is protected by paving, ballast or a green roof build up. Design life depends on the product, the exposure and the detailing, and we will tell you the expected life of whatever we quote.

What is the difference between a membrane and a water stop?

A membrane sits on the surface and keeps water off the structure. A water stop is cast inside the concrete at a joint, so water cannot track through the joint itself. Below ground you usually need both, and they have to be detailed together to work as one system.

Do you supply producer statements for council?

Yes. We issue a producer statement for the waterproofing on completion and register the manufacturer warranty for the system we installed, so your council paperwork and your warranty both line up.

Can you work to a staged build programme?

Yes. Tanking and water stop usually go in across several pours, so we stage our crews around your programme and sign off each stage before the next trade starts. Keeping the next trade off our back is part of the job.

Do you fix existing leaks, or only new builds?

Both. A good share of our work is remedial. We find where the water is actually getting in, which is often not where it shows up inside, then repair or overlay with a liquid or sheet system suited to the existing substrate.

Which system is best for a flat roof or deck?

There is no single answer. Torch on suits roofs and decks that need durability and generous laps. TPO single ply is light, reflective and heat welded. Liquid membranes handle tricky detailing and remedial work. We match the system to your falls, your penetrations and your finish.

Do you work outside central Auckland?

Yes. We cover the wider Auckland region, from the North Shore through the central suburbs and eastern bays, out west and south to Manukau and Franklin. Tell us where the site is and we will confirm before we quote.
