Auckland Waterproofing and Water Stop

Auckland Waterproofing Specialists for Membranes, Tanking and Water Stop

Auckland Integrity Waterproofing has been doing waterproofing in Auckland since 2012, on new builds, renovations and remedial work. We install certified membrane systems to flat roofs, decks, balconies, planter boxes and basements, and we carry approved applicator status with the major New Zealand suppliers so the manufacturer warranty behind your Auckland waterproofing stands up.

Water stop is the part most people never see. A waterstop is the seal cast inside the concrete at a construction joint, an expansion joint or a slab to wall junction, and it is what stops water tracking through the joint once the building is finished. We install both PVC and hydrophilic water stop alongside our tanking packages, and we detail the two together so the membrane and the joint seal work as one system rather than two separate trades.

We cover the wider Auckland region, from the North Shore and Takapuna through the central suburbs, Remuera and the eastern bays, out to West Auckland and south to Manukau. If you are weighing up which waterproofing system an Auckland project needs, send us the plans and we will tell you what we would specify and why.
