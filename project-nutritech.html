Project Overview

Nutritech International

This project had an existing factory which required an extension to the rear of the site. Joining a new roof to the existing steel cladding required an internal gutter which was made from plywood.

Each gutter was laid with a torch on two layer system, and careful attention was taken between new and old materials to ensure a watertight system.

The Challenge

Where New Meets Old

A new roof had to tie into existing steel cladding on a working factory. That junction is exactly where water finds its way in, and the two materials move and age at different rates.

The Solution

A Plywood Internal Gutter

An internal gutter was formed in plywood to carry the water at the junction, then laid in a torch on two layer system. Every transition between the new and the existing materials was detailed by hand.

Viking General Membrane laid over the plywood gutter in two layers, for the redundancy a gutter needs.

  • Laps set well clear of the water line

The Transition Detail

The join between the new roof and the existing steel cladding took the most attention on this job.

  • Membrane dressed into the existing cladding
  • Allowance for differential movement
  • Checked for watertightness before sign off
  • Factory kept running throughout
