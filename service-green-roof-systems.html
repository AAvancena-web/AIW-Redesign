Roof Gardens and Planters

Green Roof Waterproofing in Auckland

Roof gardens carry deep soil, mature planting and irrigation. We protect the structure and the living spaces underneath with a root resistant build up designed for the load that is going on top of it.

The waterproofing under a green roof has to do two things an ordinary roof membrane does not. It has to resist roots, which will find any weakness and open it, and it has to be right first time, because everything above it has to come off to reach it.

That means the build up matters as much as the membrane. Drainage and filter layers have to be specified for the depth of soil and the planting going in, and the whole thing has to be coordinated with the landscaper before anything goes down. We would rather have that conversation early than be called back into a finished garden.

How We Do It

Everything Above It Has to Come Off to Reach It

Which is the whole argument for doing it properly the first time.

The membrane

Root Resistant by Specification

Roots will find any weakness and open it, so the system is chosen for that rather than adapted to it.

  • Root resistant membranes that will not be breached
  • Root barrier where the build up calls for it
  • Upstands taken above the finished soil level
  • Outlets and inspection points kept accessible
The build up

Drainage, Filter and Load

The layers above the membrane decide whether water leaves the garden and whether the structure can carry it.

  • Drainage layer specified for the soil depth
  • Filter fabric so the drainage does not silt up
  • Coordinated with the landscaper before anything goes down
  • Sized for the weight of saturated soil

Commonly Asked

Green Roof Systems, Answered Straight

The questions we get asked most about this system. There are more on the FAQ page.

Can you install a green roof?

Yes. The waterproofing under a green roof has to be root resistant and it has to be right the first time, because everything above it has to come off to reach it. We install the membrane and the root barrier and coordinate with whoever is doing the planting above.

Will roots get through the waterproofing?

Not through a root resistant system installed properly. That is exactly what the specification is for, and where the build up calls for it we add a separate root barrier as well.

Do you waterproof planter boxes as well as full roof gardens?

Yes. Built in planters and raised beds are the same problem at a smaller scale, and they leak for the same reasons when the detailing at the top of the upstand is wrong.

Who coordinates the drainage and the planting?

We specify and install the waterproofing, the root barrier and the drainage and filter layers, and we work in with your landscaper so it all goes down in the right order.
